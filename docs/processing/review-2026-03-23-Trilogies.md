## BOOK 1: "THE REVEN" — Key Plot Events

4. Opens in ADMIN deposit lab — druid being drained of blood.
5. Jay Caster: alteration mage, whitening stubble, blue-hinted blood, 17 years at helpdesk, desk on 13th floor.
6. Jay alters matter (ceiling to dust, floors to liquid, windows to sand).
7. Dae: orphan, new hire, discovered MAP-positive by ADMIN blood test.

9. Cassandra: clairvoyant temporal mage, Jay's desk neighbor for years. Left before ADMIN arrived.

12. They fly Khathrey's aerial mobile to Nenhira's tavern. Nenhira negotiates passage for Khathrey's estate + hovercraft.
13. They leave Pearl City through the main gate.

15. They travel to Oyan (Lake Oya) and meet Krisa — former curator of the Arcanem, copied all records before it was decommissioned.

27. Closing: Dae rescues Krisa from ADMIN torture prison with "a young woman in red short robes, hands ablaze."

28. Set TA 2053 in Vikren.

30. Lucid nightmares, waking visions, suicide attempts — always revived healed.

35. They lead the offensive against ADMIN. Discover ADMIN is manipulated by ancient evil spirit "R."

38. They head to Isle of Thuunrow to assault dredth fortress led by Hexrad.
39. Meet Notan, leader of Thunra. Massive cannons fire on the fortress. Dae sends bomber drones and proposes cannon improvements.

43. Find letter "Secret of Amanor" in deepest cave.

47. Tenebris joins — kills a Vickkhamendrog. Pale man, black eyes, kind smile. Domin's son, Haeres' half-brother.
48. Goadlith captured — carries changeroots that turn people into half-monsters.
49. College of Magic still exists (Headmaster greets Irys).
50. Attacked by enslaved villagers under mind spell.

52. Irys shrinks Zohlr with energy drain spell. Jit'al rescues miniature Zohlr.
53. Carnifex kidnaps Tenebris. Vlho transforms into dragon. Heroes flee south on Tyn's rockets. Vlho destroys Lonun.
54. Em'Faira aids heroes in forest of Fairen. Reveals Tenebris' parentage and the Chaos Empire.
55. Heroes force Haeres to portal away weakened.
56. Dae appears with machines. Lonun badly damaged but survivors exist.
57. Tortured Tenebris falls through portal nearly dead. Reveals he told enemy the oblivion stone's location.
58. Dae: stone is empty (Valnarey holds the power), but they want the stone itself to capture energy to summon Umbra.
59. Final assault: fleet of airships/land vessels vs. Dredth Fortress. Thunrow artillery (improved by Dae) fires from east.
60. In Pearl City: Dae vs. Exsecratus + revived Witch Queen + witches.
61. Individual final matchups: Armenia vs. Carnifex, Tyn vs. Vlho, Irys vs. Zohlr, Curse vs. Jit'al, Tenebris vs. Haeres, Dae vs. Exsecratus, Valnarey vs. Umbra (through Domin).
62. Valnarey uses soul magic to prevent the stone from opening. Dae directs Umbra's energy into the stone. Valnarey pushes Umbra inside.
63. Dae boxes the stone in a unique prison in Pearl City.
64. Dae's final speech: "Umbra and the evil dark forces of chaos have been defeated!"

## OLD WAR TRILOGY — Key Events

65. **Shadow's Reach**: Umbra's Shadow Legion invades from Nulthara. Divines (Amae, Aera/Eyra, Nova) descend to guide mortals. Nova sacrifices herself to seal the rift. Revenant Stone created to imprison Umbra's essence.
66. **The Wicked Queen**: Undrea infiltrates Nildon, assassinates Queen Elenna (tore out throat, drank blood). Declares herself Queen of Mayrn. Leia, Xyrax, Ir lead resistance. Ir uses Everflame Forge for enchanted weapons. Leia destroys Blood Chalice. Undrea's dying curse: "Your blood shall be your undoing. Your unity shall be your death." Andrea (disillusioned Nil mage) joins Undrea, then flees after her fall.
67. **Magic Wars**: Nildon becomes human stronghold under Technocratic Assembly, banning magic. Mages retreat to Arcanem. Andrea becomes blood witch leading warlocks. Three-way battle at Arcanem. Xyrax sacrifices himself to stabilize magical rift. Nildon renamed Pearl City; walls built. Mages scatter. Andrea and warlocks retreat to Enul.
