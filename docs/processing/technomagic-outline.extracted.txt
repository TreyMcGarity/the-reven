The Reven
Book 1 Outline (Act by Act)
ACT 0 — Prologue
Location: ADMIN deposit lab, Pearl City
Unnamed druid, wizard, and witch — chained/drained. None named, none reappear.
Sets tone: ongoing before the story starts. Lyann Harbor studies a vial of blood.
ACT I — Pearl City
Location: Pearl City (PTC office, streets, alleys)
Jay Caster, character is introduced and we follow him until he meets Dae on his first day. ADMIN sweep outs Dae; Jay breaks cover, they escape.
Cassandra joins. They go to Khathrey's estate. Leads them to Nenhira's den — deal for passage out, Cassandra gambles for SC, Dae buys a busted HeadWave.
Limney hands Khathrey the wrapped Revenant Stone (stolen from Nenhira) on the way out.
Exit Pearl City toward Crossroads.
ACT II — The Old World
River's Bend — rapids, rescue, inn stay, Krisa is there investigating the Dark Forest - joins them Coast past Amanor ruins → Wenyora — sea serpent; Khathrey reveals the Stone's history; repair lead points to Atawa. Atawa — Tash repairs the HeadWave; witch coven detects unclassifiable magic in Dae's blood, notes his bloodline "eludes" them; Dae's first awakening frees the group; Nenhira's ambush; Tash brokers ADMIN truce; Lyann confronts them — reveals Cassandra's family paid for protection and got captured too; portal back to Oyan.
ACT III — North
Oyan → Crossroads — Khathrey calls in a favor with a crime family for safe passage north. Campfire, on the road — Khathrey explains her Dosram heritage and daughter; theorizes Dae's bloodline traces to the side that fled north. Briskden — the guarded pass, border tension. Dosramgree → Jolhran → NW Mountains — cave temple. Here Dae, now attuned to his powers, senses the deeper truth: Genak commands the dark spirit secretly steering the ADMIN. This reframes the whole hunt — Genak isn't just a monster, it's the reason ADMIN exists as it does.
Battle against Genak; Khathrey's dark-arts knowledge (and her Dosram-linked history with the Stone) proves decisive — she absorbs her demon into the Revenant Stone.
Jay Caster dies at the cave entrance.
Defeating Genak weakens but doesn't destroy the spirit controlling ADMIN — sets up Book 2's stakes.
Krisa opens the escape portal, stays behind — captured.
Closing
ADMIN black site, Pearl City — Dae and the group breach it, rescue Cassandra’s family and Krisa. "Jay is dead... the Technomancer is going to change the world after all, but they need a powerful mage with Kathrey’s abilities to be able to change the tide of the conflict between Mages and ADMIN - Dae decides they need Khathrey’s daughter Valna”
