## THE NILVAR & THE DARK FOREST (New Lore)

### Origin of the Nilvar
- The Nilvar are NOT Dred/Nullith sap-creatures. They are **corrupted Inil people**.
- A different corruption exists: ore deep in a mountain + a dark spirit that darkens the forest and corrupts the people.
- Inil who separated into the woods became hollow, corrupted people → the Nilvar.
- They serve Umbra willingly. Intelligent but small population.
- Not currently active in the game era — designed to be easily added later.
- Em'Faira is the dominant dark spirit influencing the forest.

### Geography — The Dark Forest
- Located south of Inilgree, also south of Enulgree (forest borders both regions from the south).
- Contains a city called **Nildan** deep within the dark forest.
- The peninsula tip at the south of the continent is equidistant from Inildon and Wenyoreck.
- When people revolted against magic, some moved south to this peninsula location — able to trade and fight the Nilvar.
- Amanar's destruction may be tied to an onslaught involving the Minilta and Nilvar.

### The Ancient War
- The **Minilta** (legendary Inil army) fought the **Nilvar** in the First Age.
- Mutual destruction. A city was destroyed — possibly by Novastra to prevent Nilvar takeover.
- Both armies retreated after heavy losses.
- The Nilvar disappeared except in myth of the Inil.
- They sometimes appear in the shared forest between Inil and Enul lands.
- The Minilta were later disbanded for not supporting magic wielders.

### Naming
- **Nilvar** = corrupted Inil army (evil, from the dark forest, under Umbra)
- **Minilta** = legendary ancient Inil army (good, First Age, disbanded)
- **Inilvan** = current standard Inil army (replaced "Inilvar")
- **Nildan** = city deep in the dark forest

## FACTION ENRICHMENTS (All confirmed Y with additions)

### Inil
- Traditional kingdom in FA, tech-advanced by TA through Dae.

### Dosram
- Political separatists FIRST. Also cunning business people.
- Land scarred by Shadow Conquest, people shaped by surviving it. Hardy badasses.
- Future: currency/business skill tree, possible mini-games, game-level casino.

### Enul
- Silent rangers, hunters, huntsmen. Druid magic after Well of Mana.
- Love and respect nature. Defend themselves during Dred **Night Raids**.
- NOT pacifists — lethal when needed.

### Wend
- Currency-driven, charismatic salesmen. Commerce backbone of Mayrn.
- Cities are lawless, chaotic. Strongest captain + crew runs the show.
- Frequent mutinies. The longest-running head-captain only lasted a few months.
- Inil aristocrats (business owners) trade heavily with them.

### Thuun
- Think **Warhammer 40K Astartes**. Definition of lawful good. Powerful, skilled.
- Most successful and uncorruptable, NOT most tech-advanced.
- Thuunrow is a perfect society. Banishment = Thuunrath.

### Dred
- Sap-spawn from Nullith. NOT human. Adapt quickly.
- Older ones look bugish/creature-esque. Newer ones humanoid with wings.

## GAME DESIGN DECISIONS

### Player Character ("The Reven")
- Character menu shows made characters with loadouts/currencies.
- New character creation → camera pans to Divines on 11 thrones in Solthara.
- Divines explain briefly: player is meant to live in the world and find it worth protecting.
- Fades to player looking at statues of the thrones (depicted perfectly but not active).
- Player is one of MANY Revens (all players are Revens).
- Provided a body to interact with and participate in the world.
- Appearance: created and modifiable.
- Name: tied to player account.
- No real need for multiple characters due to gameplay freedom.

### Main Questline Path (REVISED)
1. **Inildon** (start)
2. **River's Bend** (frontline against Dred)
3. **Arcanem** (magic discovery)
4. **Crossroads** (commerce/crime families)
5. **Ashpeak** (smithing introduction, Dosram connection)
6. **Dosram up to Donrid** (northern territory)
7. **South to tallest point** (speak to Platgree)
8. **Southeast to Enul**
9. **Barrenlands** (Summoner's Sect → Central Dred Fortress = finale)

### Side Questlines
- Somewhat independent from main quest.
- World remains alive and dynamic after main quest completion.
- Evil of humanity persists — world stays challenging.

### Dungeon System (5 confirmed dungeons)

| # | Name/Location | Level | Theme | Boss |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Dark Forest (near River's Bend) | 10 | Nilvar cult, corrupted wildlife/bugs | Giant spider + resurrected Nilvar soldier |
| 2 | Amanar | 20 | Evil spirit, death/drain | Spirit that drains life — kill before drained |
| 3 | NW Dred Fortress (Dosram) | 30 | Dark, demonic magic/creatures | Dred-themed |
| 4 | Summoner's Sect | TBD | Warlock, demons, conjuration, dark spirits | Conjuration-themed |
| 5 | Central Dred Fortress | Final | Concludes main story | Final boss |

Dungeon rules:
- 30-60 minutes per run
- Encounter layout: small → medium → big boss
- Social areas at dungeon entrances for group forming
- Solo-able OR co-op with 2-4 players
- More dungeons can be added for eastern Mayrn and other locations

### Loading/Instancing
- Cities and houses load separate from the open world
- Some buildings are in the open world (seamless)
- Dungeons are instanced

### PvP System
- Some world areas repurposed for PvP
- Modes: 1v1 dueling, up to 10v10 ground war
- Game modes: Free-for-all (10 players), Team Deathmatch, Objective-based
- Any size from specified pool
- Save development effort for PvP match areas over additional dungeons

### Revenant Stone During Game Era
- Held by Ullenrey's family (Khathrey's lineage) in SECRET in Inildon.
- This is WHY Ullenrey became the first conjuration mage.
- The Summoner's Sect was formed around this responsibility.
- The stone remains in Inildon until Book 1 events (TA era), when Khathrey smuggles it out.
- During the game era (FA 10840-10843): the stone is hidden in Inildon, its existence unknown to most.

**Tone**: Skyrim atmosphere + Destiny 2 seamless multiplayer, dungeon running, challenges, and loot system.

**What makes The Reven different**: Freedom. A world that feels lived-in and worth living in. Easy to understand mechanics that reveal complexity through interest. Skyrim's exploration freedom + Destiny 2's co-op events and community. Simple to start, deep to master.
