## Faction Corrections / Enrichments

### Inil (updated)
- Become the most technologically advanced civilization through Dae's help in the Third Age.
- Military advancement specifically noted.
- First Age: traditional kingdom. Third Age: tech-advanced.

### Dosram (corrected)
- The "area" scarred by the Shadow Conquest.
- The people are AFFECTED by the Shadow Conquest — shaped by it.
- Classic hardy badasses. Not just separatists — they're survivors of darkness.

### Enul (corrected — NOT pacifists)
- Delicate hunters and gatherers.
- Silent rangers and huntsmen.
- Druid magic types when magic is created.
- NOT pacifists. They are quiet, skilled, lethal when needed.

### Wend (enriched)
- Currency-based society. Everything is transactional.
- All are charismatic salesmen.
- Handle most business, especially travel and shipping.
- Some Inil aristocrats (business owners) do heavy trade with them.
- They are the commerce backbone of Mayrn despite being "pirates."

### Thuun (clarified)
- NOT the most technologically advanced (that's Inil in TA) — but the most SUCCESSFUL and UNCORRUPTABLE race.
- Thuunrow is a perfect system.
- Anyone who abandons the society or the Divines is banished = becomes Thuunrath.
- Characters who leave Thuunrow before the official declaration of action are acting out of orders — creates complexity of duty vs. loyalty.
- The Lost Thuun (Bek's crew) turned against the Divines in name = Thuunrath by choice.

### Dred (clarified — NOT human)
- Coagulated and hardened saps from the Nullith tree.
- Morph into spawn of whatever they can mimic.
- Species adapts quickly.
- Eventually develop wings, humanoid forms, etc.
- Older versions look bugish and creature-esque.
- They are NOT former humans — they are sap-spawn that evolved to mimic human traits.
