# Design Decisions — Gap Responses (2026-03-23)

Status: These are **human-approved design decisions** pending formal canonization.

## Resolved Gaps

### 1. Divine Favor Stat
**Decision**: REMOVED from final draft. Only 5 stats remain.
**Impact**: All references to Divine Favor in design docs should be removed. Stat system is now: Might, Fortitude, Haste, Agility, Sorcery.

### 2. 6th Guild/House
**Decision**: REMOVED. Guilds might be cut entirely. Definitely not in first iteration.
**Impact**: Guild system is deferred. If implemented later, only 5 guilds max (matching 5 stats).

### 3. Dosramgree Scope
**Decision**: NOT in first build. Potential first expansion. Intended as a neutral zone. Human wants to see how PvP/PvE implementation works out first.
**Impact**: Dosramgree is deferred to post-launch expansion. Map boundaries stop before Dosram territory.

### 4. PvP Scope
**Decision**: YES, included. A few maps for matches, reusable for different game modes. Not absolutely necessary but valued for game longevity with co-op/PvP for playing with friends.
**Impact**: PvP maps and basic matchmaking are in scope. Not the highest priority but should be designed alongside PvE.

### 5. Max Player Level
**Decision**: Level 100. At max Might, player can use a two-handed weapon as one-handed.
**Impact**: Progression curve must be designed for 100 levels. Milestone unlocks (like 2H-as-1H) need to be mapped.

### 6. Arcane Magic Tier
**Decision**: Wards may belong in a separate group. Much of the "arcane" magic is phased out as inherent abilities (like auto-responding). Can be implemented more seriously later.
**Impact**: Arcane is not a formal spell tier. Ward-type effects may be passive/inherent rather than equipped spells. Defer detailed arcane spell system.

### 7. Online Menu
**Decision**: Simple PvP or dungeon menu. Queue-based group selection for events. Players can invite friends.
**Impact**: Online menu = matchmaking queue (PvP maps + co-op dungeons) with party invite system.

### 8. ChatGPT Suggestions
**Decision**: Human is unsure which ChatGPT-generated suggestions to accept. Needs further review.
**Impact**: Flag all ChatGPT-sourced content as UNRESOLVED until human reviews each suggestion individually.

### 9. Back Gear Slot
**Decision**: The Back slot is a weapon slot reserved for two-handed weapons ONLY. Weapon system is: Main Hand / Off Hand / Back. Back is for 2H weapons. Main+Off can also hold a 2H weapon.
**Impact**: Player can carry two weapon setups: main/off + back (2H). Weapon switching between these loadouts.

### 10. Damage Scaling Formula
**Decision**: TBD. Wants simplest possible formula but needs good PvP scaling. PvP needs a corrective calibration to equalize the field.
**Impact**: Damage formula is UNRESOLVED. Design team should propose a simple base formula with a PvP normalization modifier.

### 11. Player Death Mechanics
**Decision**: Skyrim-style for PvE (reload last save). Wants best respawn mechanism for both PvE and PvP.
**Impact**: PvE = save/load checkpoint system. PvP = respawn system (to be designed). Design team should propose PvP respawn mechanics.

### 12. Enchanting Scale
**Decision**: Emulate Skyrim. Player must find/learn an enchantment first. Can disenchant enchanted equipment to save the buff/debuff. Enchanting level determines usefulness. Costs essence + gold.
**Impact**: Enchanting progression = learn enchantments → disenchant items to extract → apply to gear using essence + gold → level determines quality.

### 13. Spell Unlock Schedule
**Decision**: Two paths: (1) Find spell tomes scattered throughout the world, (2) Progress the Arcanem questline which unlocks special spell vendors.
**Impact**: Spells are not level-gated directly. They're gated by exploration (tomes) and quest progression (Arcanem vendors).

### 14. "Quild"
**Decision**: Typo for "guild."
