## CHANGES

### Materials & Crafting
- Materials will be diverse but simplified: generic minerals and ore types
- Different visual assets for different armor rarity tiers with better stats
- Smithing system improves gear quality
- Enchantment system **affects playstyle** (not just stat boosts — changes how you play)
- Keep material system manageable for solo dev

### Spells
- Most spells from Make it a Game.docx are **PHASED OUT**
- Remaining spells need to align to the confirmed 10-school magic system
- Spell list needs full review and remediation against: Flora, Earth, Fire, Water, Wild, Restoration, Temporal, Soul, Necromancy, Conjuration, Blood
- Arcane/Ward remains deferred (may become inherent abilities)

### Guilds & Houses
- **Guilds are CUT completely.**
- **Houses** = PvP friend/clan associations with:
  - Extra benefits for certain tasks
  - Group improvements
  - Social/competitive structure for multiplayer

### Mounts
- **REMOVED completely.** No mounts in any phase.
