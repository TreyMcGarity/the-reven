## PART 4: EVENT CONFLICTS

### Conflict 1: Linear Year Math -- Systematic +10 Offset
**Problem**: The formula states SA + 10,843 = Linear year, but CURRENT_STATE.md consistently shows values that are +10 higher than this calculation.

| Event | SA Year | Computed (SA+10843) | CURRENT_STATE Linear | Discrepancy |
|-------|---------|---------------------|---------------------|-------------|
| Magic Wars begin | SA 2886 | 13,729 | 13,739 | +10 |
| Wicked War begins | SA 3579 | 14,422 | 14,432 | +10 |
| Wicked War ends | SA 7443 | 18,286 | 18,296 | +10 |
| Witch Queen to Grul'Ukruin | SA 8147 | 18,990 | 19,000 | +10 |
| Magic Wars end | SA 9963 | 20,806 | 20,816 | +10 |

**Analysis**: The consistent +10 suggests the SA-to-Linear offset should be **10,853** (not 10,843), OR there is a 10-year gap between FA 10843 and SA 0, OR there is a systematic error in the source. Canonized in CANON-HIST-031 as a known issue deferred to later reconciliation.

**Recommendation**: Decide whether FA ends at 10843 and SA begins at 10853 (with a 10-year transition), or adjust the offset formula.

### Conflict 2: Quenla and Morhri -- Dual Roles?
**Problem**: The raw Lore.docx describes Quenla as "the appointed military leader under Nova" and Morhri as "the Lead strategist" in Solthara among the Sentries. But the canon character files list them as mortal Inil leaders (Commander of Inilvar, lead strategist on Inil Advisory Council).

**Possible Resolution**: They may serve in both capacities -- spiritual Sentry roles AND mortal military roles. Or the raw doc may be describing them as mortal leaders who are spiritually appointed by Nova.

**Recommendation**: Author should clarify whether Quenla and Morhri are:
1. Mortal leaders who also serve as Sentry commanders in Solthara
2. Mortal leaders whose earthly roles mirror divine appointments
3. Conflated references that should be separated

**Recommendation**: Author should decide if Zyrax dies at the fall of the Arcanem or if he survives (perhaps via temporal magic).
