## WORLD STRUCTURE

### Three Realms
- **Solthara** (First Realm): Divine realm, realm of light. Home of the eleven Divines, spirits, Sentries, and angel types.
- **Nulthara** (Second Realm): Outer darkness. Umbra's domain. Home of the Shadow Legion and Chaos Empire.
- **Subthara** (Third Realm): Mortal realm / Earth. Contains continent Mayrn and others.

### Continents
- **Mayrn**: Primary continent. Home to Inil, Dosram, Enul, Wend, Thuun, Dred.
- **Dal'lan**: Southwest. Home to Ralli'Omai (humans + giant ants) and giant insects.
- **Grul'Ukruin**: Southern continent. Mecca-Hein (orc-like people) under Undrea's rule.
- **Altir**: Far east, largest continent. Home to Ven'Altir mushroom species (Wei, Ljos, Dokk) and the Thuunrath.
- **Array / Tri-Isles**: East of Mayrn coast. Arra seafarers.

## MAYRN FACTIONS

### Inil (formerly "Nil")
- First people, devout and regal. Capital: Inildon (becomes Pearl City in TA).
- Elegant steel armor with celestial engravings. Favor swords, spears, halberds.
- Gender roles: women own homes/lead government, men own companies/community work.
- Army: The Inilvar. Commander: Quenla.
- Region: Inilgree.

### Dosram
- Northern separatists. Capital: Donrid (destroyed during Shadow Conquest).
- Democratic republic with elected Tutors. Founded by Rien Highwind.
- Hardy, honor-bound. Heavy furs and iron armor.
- **Deferred to post-launch expansion** for game purposes.

### Enul
- Eastern mystics, hunters, druids. Main community: Lonun.
- Underground homes in grassy mounds. Natural materials, traps, secrecy.
- Led by Greysong family ("Young Eld" title).

### Wend
- Southern pirates. Capital: Wenyoreck (at Wendo Bay).
- Chaotic neutral. Black-sailed vessels. "Strongest captain wins."

### Thuun
- Reclusive eastern lords. Isle of Thuunrow.
- Most technologically advanced people. Polished steel plate with glowing runes.
- Quadite council (People/Place/Product/Passed).
- Barely involved in world affairs until recruited by Dae in TA.

### Dred
- Shadow-mutated creatures spawned from the Nullith (dark tree planted by Umbra).
- Bleed black. Originally blind, cannibalistic cave dwellers. Adapted human traits over millennia.
- Types: Minions, Soldiers, Tankers, Woldreds, Dregs, Draiths, Dralls, Draids, Draiders.
- Fortresses: Central (Barrenlands), Western (Donrid), Eastern (hidden until TA).

## MAGIC SYSTEM

### Origin
- Well of Mana created by the Divines (led by Mortyn's design).
- 64 original mages drank from it. Undrea jumped in last, destroying the well.

### Schools (10 confirmed)
| Tier | Schools |
|---|---|
| **Terrestrial** | Flora, Earth, Fire, Water, Wild |
| **Astral** | Restoration, Temporal, Soul |
| **Baleful** | Necromancy, Conjuration, Blood |

**Note**: Wind is NOT a formal school. Olrean is canonically Nature/Floral (narrative claims of Wind/Water/Rune are incorrect — deleted). Arcane/Ward is deferred as a formal tier — may become inherent abilities.

### Magic Users
- **Mages**: Blend with non-magical communities. Generally good-aligned.
- **Warlocks**: Dark/conjuration focus. Summoner's Sect was the largest faction.
- **Witches**: Dark/blood focus. Undrea was the first and most powerful.

## GAME DESIGN — CURRENT VISION

### Core Stats (5)
| Stat | Effect | Primary Armor |
|---|---|---|
| Might | Damage output, fight abilities | Head |
| Fortitude | Damage reduction, shield, health regen | Body |
| Haste | Movement speed, stamina regen | Boots |
| Agility | Attack speed, dodge/dash | Gloves |
| Sorcery | Spell cast speed, mana regen | Legs |

### Dynamic Stats (3)
- Health (base 100), Stamina (base 100), Mana (base 100)

### Gear
- **5 armor slots**: Head, Body, Gloves, Legs, Boots
- **3 weapon slots**: Main Hand, Off Hand, Back (2H weapons only)
- **5 spell slots**
- Random roll system for stat diversity on gear

### Combat Triangle
- **Melee**: High damage, limited range, stamina-based. Parry/riposte.
- **Ranged**: Bows, crossbows, thrown. Projectile physics, ammo management.
- **Magic**: 5 equipped spells. Cooldown-based. Sorcery scaling.
- **Stamina Break**: Full depletion = 1-2 sec exhaustion, halved regen.

### Progression
- **Max level**: 100
- **Spell acquisition**: Tomes in world + Arcanem questline vendors
- **Crafting**: Smithing (materials), Alchemy (potions/food), Enchanting (Skyrim-style: learn → disenchant → apply with essence + gold)
- **Death**: PvE = Skyrim-style save/reload. PvP = respawn (TBD).

### Multiplayer
- **Co-op**: Amanor dungeon post-main-quest. Flexible roles.
- **PvP**: Separate maps with queue system + party invites. Not critical path.
- **Dosramgree**: Deferred to first expansion (neutral zone concept).
- **Guilds/Houses**: Deferred, possibly cut.

### Vertical Slice
- **Location**: River's Bend
- **Content**: Basic melee combat, one Dred camp dungeon with mini-boss, magic discovery sequence
- **Cap**: Level 10 with basic skills in one faction tree

## DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

The game should embody:
- **Accessible depth**: Simple to grasp, complexity reveals through interest
- **Player freedom**: Weapon/armor system empowers personal playstyle
- **Living world**: Feels lived-in and worth living in
- **Longevity**: PvE + PvP + co-op for lasting engagement
- **Inclusivity**: New players never locked out; veterans find depth
- **Well-implemented scaling**: Damage formula must work for both PvE and PvP with corrective calibration
