## COMBAT SYSTEM

36. Melee: close-range with physical weapons. Combos, stamina-based.
37. Ranged: bows, crossbows, thrown weapons. Projectile physics, ammo management, charge mechanics.
38. Magic: 5 equipped spells (Q, E, R, F + 5th slot TBD). Cooldowns. Stat scaling via Sorcery.
39. Parry/Block: reduces damage. Timed parry staggers enemies.
40. Stamina Break: full depletion = 1-2 sec exhaustion with halved regen.
41. Weapon types: Fists, Swords, Axes, Maces (either hand), Sceptres (main only), Shields (off only), Heavy Weapons and Glaives (both hands).
42. Hit detection: Sphere/Capsule Trace during attack animation.
43. Damage types: fire (DoT), frost (slow), lightning (chain).
44. Elemental synergies: Earth+Fire (Quicksand ignites), Temporal+Arcane (time-slow + reflect).
45. Hybrid builds: Battle Mages (melee+elemental), Magic Archers (mana-infused arrows).
46. Artifacts remove weapon use, act as spell slots. Dual artifacts = 2 extra spell slots, forfeit physical combat.
