## 1. Novastra's "Sacrifice"
- Novastra was **wounded**, NOT sacrificed/killed.
- She is still one of the most powerful Divines.
- The Trilogies.docx Old War Trilogy outline claiming she "sacrificed herself" is **INCORRECT** — should be revised to "was gravely wounded sealing the rift."
- She remains active as leader of the Sentry defense in Solthara.

## 2. Zyrax's Lifespan
- All mages with mana in their blood have **extended lifetimes**.
- Normal mortal lifespan is already 100+ years.
- Mana extends this significantly — Zyrax living 3,000+ years as a temporal mage is consistent with world rules.
- This also explains: Undrea's longevity (blood magic), Khathrey's longevity (conjuration/dark energy), Shades' longevity, and other long-lived mages.
- **Canon rule**: Mana in the blood extends mortal lifespan. Stronger mages / darker magic = longer life.

## 3. Nilvar During Game Era (FA 10840-10843)
- **YES**, the Nilvar are active but **weakened** after the destruction of Amanar.
- The destruction of Amanar should be **recent history** at game start — not ancient.
- **Donrid** gets attacked at the beginning of the game.
- The player hears about Donrid's attack through NPCs naturally.
- **World state changes with progression** — NPCs are aware of events as they happen.
- Similar to Skyrim's Civil War questline where cities change based on quest progress.

### Dynamic World State System
- NPCs react to world events as the player progresses.
- Cities change state based on story progression.
- News of events (Donrid attack, Dred movements, faction responses) spreads through NPC dialogue.
- This creates a living world that responds to the player's progress through the main questline.
