The Reven
Book 3: The Reven — Chaos (revised outline, updated)
ACT 0
2256TA, south Enulgree. Armenia, a small child, is attacked by a Jathrip while tending sheep — loses her right arm in the mauling. Her mother carries her, dying, to a Pearl City scout camp passing through. Pearl City medics stabilize her; days later, a Pearl City engineer fits her with a growing robotic arm, tied to nerve-response tech, explicitly the product of Dae’s post-Book-2 tech-sharing initiative. She grows up in her village, eventually leaving after her mother’s death to fight monsters and help travelers.
(Interspersed cut-ins: Dae and Valnarey, decades into their partnership, investigating scattered Dredth activity across the continent — establishing them as active and visible throughout, not offscreen.)
ACT I — Gathering the Company
Armenia, now grown, partners with Tyn on a bounty. They pick up Irys in Nehren, then Curse near Pearl City. Together they clear the Cave Wrevens and find the “Secret of Amanor” letter.
Cut-in: Dae and Valnarey, elsewhere, tracking the same Dredth fortress network the letter references.
At Amanor, ambushed by a draid-led Dredth force; escape to River’s Bend, meet Weinfra Lightbane and the Hallowed Knights. Hear of the Vickkhamendrog bounty, meet Tenebris.
Cut-in: Dae and Valnarey confront a lesser Immoral scout, learn Domin is active near Fairen.
ACT II — The Widening War
Company attacked by half-monster hybrids; capture Goadlith, follow the trail through the College of Magic, discover an enslaved village under mind-control. Reach Lonun, warn of the incoming Dredth army.
Cut-in: Dae and Valnarey at a separate front (Barrenlands), Valnarey has her own direct confrontation with an Immoral, showing continued growth in parallel.
Attack on Lonun: company defends the city against Zohlr and Jit’al; Irys shrinks Zohlr but he’s rescued. City holds, badly damaged.
Company ambushed in the Barrenlands by Vlho and Carnifex; Tenebris captured. Retreat to Fairen, aided by Em’faira, who reveals Tenebris’s parentage and Domin’s scheme. Confront Haeres, force its retreat.
Convergence: Dae meets the company in Fairen’s aftermath, revealing he and Valnarey have been shadowing the same conflict from the start. Brings them to Lonun, where Valnarey is already stationed — reunion establishes both as integrated allies going into Act III.
ACT III — The Fortress
Coalition planning in Lonun: Hallowed Knights, Atawen, Thunra, Dosram forces. Tenebris escapes captivity — having been tortured for the Revenant Stone’s location — and warns the enemy seeks it to revive the Witch Queen and summon Umbra.
Dae departs for Pearl City to protect the Stone personally; Valnarey stays with the company for the main assault, at Dae’s request.
Pearl City, parallel thread: Dae arrives too late — Exsecratus and Haeres have already breached the vault and taken the Revenant Stone, fleeing with the revived Witch Queen and her coven in tow, carrying the Stone to Domin at the fortress. Dae engages the coven and mechanized forces buy time but can’t stop the extraction; Haeres portals Exsecratus and the Stone out before Dae can intervene. Dae immediately follows, racing to reach the fortress before Domin can use it.
Company departs for the Dredth fortress; massed battle (Thunrow artillery, Atawen reinforcements, Thunra soldiers).
Fortress interior, paired duels:
•Armenia v. Carnifex
•Tyn v. Vlho
•Irys v. Zohlr
•Curse v. Jit’al
•Tenebris v. Haeres
•Valnarey v. Domin (channeling Umbra, now in possession of the Stone)
Climax: Domin, holding the Stone delivered by Exsecratus, attempts to open it to channel Umbra through. Valnarey uses soul magic to seal it instead. Dae arrives in time (having chased the Stone from Pearl City) and uses his tech to redirect Umbra’s energy back into the Stone. Umbra is trapped; the Stone is recovered and secured back at Pearl City.
Closing
Victory at Lonun. Dae’s speech on the new age of possibility. Company established as the next generation’s Hallowed Knights-equivalent
