## COSMOLOGY (27 claims)

1. Before all existence, an opaque mist stretched across an endless plane.
2. Energy rippled through the mist, condensing into particles that grew into unbound entities.
3. Some entities grew large with a magnificent blaze (became the Divines); many smaller ones (became spirits).
4. Nine of the greatest energies gravitated together and met at a center.
5. The first language was "the most subtle song of hums."
6. The greater entities declared themselves "The Divines" and gave themselves names.
7. The smaller entities were called "spirits" (varied behavior: self-aware, wandering, or motionless).
8. Known space declared "the Realm" (later "First Realm").
9. Umbra uncloaked from the emptiness beyond the Divines' light — a monstrous entity of dark energy.
10. Umbra already knew all Divines by name before revealing itself.
11. Umbra called out specific Divines with different attitudes (hatred for Amae, respect for Mortyki, dismissal of Laewu/Platgree/Kolvarin/Stellan/Irial).
12. Only agreement between Divines and Umbra: "Existence is made of constantly changing and contrasting energies."
13. Umbra refused Amae's proposed order; declared chaos the natural state.
14. Self-aware smaller spirits demanded a voice in governance.
15. Vaeya spoke out, gained alliance of united spirits, became the "Allmother."
16. Umbra departed with followers into the outer darkness — the "Second Realm."
17. Before disappearing, Umbra pinched the threshold, creating a black hole.
18. The black hole was compressed by the mist of the universe into The Revenant Stone — a pearlescent gem with flickers of the abyss.
19. The Divines hid the Revenant Stone from the outer darkness.
20. Three Realms: First/Solthara (Divine/light), Second/Nulthara (outer darkness/Umbra), Third/Subthara (mortal/Earth/Mayrn).
21. "Before the Ages" = period before creation of Earth.
22. The Nulthara steals energy, led by Umbra.
23. Divines created the Subthara/Earth for spirits to live.
24. Tolen created Time as a tool to fix entities to a linear path.
25. Nova created a shield of stars between Third and Second Realms.
26. A beacon (Sun) placed where Umbra had stood; Vaeya housed herself there.
27. Moon created by Platgree at Amae's request — "As there are shadows in the light, there must be light in the shadows."

## THE NINE DIVINES (11 claims)

28. **Vaeya** (Allmother): leader of spirits, housed in the Sun, crafted mortal form, created first people.
29. **Mortyki/Mortyn**: most intelligent Divine, designed Mayrn architecture and mortal brains, devised Mana.
30. **Amae**: devoted to balance, proclaimed order. Only Divine who wanted Umbra released post-Chaos Skirmish.
31. **Nova/Novastra**: star goddess, created star shield, led Sentries, carries a glaive.
32. **Tolen**: devised Time, controls access between realms.
33. **Eyra**: silent/mysterious, wears a cowl. Specific domain never stated.
34. **Kolvarin/Kolvari**: elemental Divine (storms, oceans, wind, fire, ice). Childish and joyful. Earthen Divine.
35. **Platgree**: created solid worlds and Earth. Shrank after creating the Moon. Rests on favorite mountain. Earthen Divine.
36. **Stellan**: wild Divine, prefers wild forms, created creatures/terrain with Irial. Fights in stag form. Earthen Divine.
37. **Irial**: silent Divine, adored peaceful spirits, worked with Stellan on nature. Weeping goddess. Earthen Divine.
38. **Laewu/Leawu**: holds one truth — let things freely change. Known for sleeping. Poisoned by Empra.
