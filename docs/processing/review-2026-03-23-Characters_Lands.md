## DIVINE REALM (SOLTHARA)

1. Nine Divines govern: Vaeya (Matriarchy/Allmother), Amae (Order), Novastra (Light/Stars), Eyra (Virtue), Tolen (Continuance/Time), Mortyn (Intellect), Platgree (Land), Irial (Nature), Stellan (Wildlife), Kolvari (Elements), Laewu (Idle).
2. Some spirits never go to Earth and remain as "Angels" (derogatorily called that).
3. Sentries are both angels and returned mortal spirits who defend both realms.
4. Angel types: Peace (join Laewu's parties), Harmony (join Eyra's choir), Wisdom (join Mortyn as astral explorers), Lux (defector Sentry who breaks the barrier).

6. Shadow Legion: T (Dark Spirit, Shadow Magic), K (Dark Spirit, Shadow Magic).

Chaos Empire members: Domin (Clad of Umbra, Demon), Carnifex (Demon, Torture), V (Dark Spirit, Life Siphon), Vlho (Monster, Shape Shifter), Goadlith (Monster, Recruiter/Changeroot), Genak (Monster, absorbed by Khathrey's Demon then by Khathrey), R (Dark Spirit, manipulates ADMIN), Hexrad (Demon, Enhanced Draid), Tenebris (Demon, ascends from evil and joins heroes).
