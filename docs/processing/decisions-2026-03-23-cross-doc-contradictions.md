## Resolved

### 1. Caia's Surname
- **Heartune** (Lore.docx) vs. **Morningsong** (Characters_Lands.docx)
- **Decision**: **Morningsong** is correct. Characters_Lands.docx has the most recent names.

### 2. Bek vs. Akrin
- Bek Akrin (Thuun lost captain who went East to Altir) vs. Akrin (mysterious warrior who led Wend fighters in Enul during Shadow Conquest)
- **Decision**: **Different people.** Akrin is a separate pivotal character. Bek is the lost Thuun captain. The name similarity is coincidental or a lineage connection to be explored later.

### 3. Leia vs. Lea the Primuma
- **Decision**: **Same person.** Leia is Lea the Primuma — the first person to drink from the Well of Mana, master restorationist, leader of the mages of the Nilvar, who expelled Umbra and mortally wounded the Witch Queen.

### 4. Em'Faira's Allegiance
- Listed as an Immortal (Dark Spirit, Corrupt Nature Magic) in Characters_Lands.docx but aids the heroes in Trilogies Book 3.
- **Decision**: **Villain turned hero.** Em'Faira is a reformed Immortal who switched sides and actively helps the heroes against the Chaos Empire.

### 5. Engine Decision
- Make it a Game.docx specifies UE5. Architecture recommended Unity.
- **Decision**: **Unreal Engine 5.** This is the formally approved engine choice.
